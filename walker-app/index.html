Walk grades

E · Easy
Gentle walking on good paths.

Some climbing; a steady pace for the day.

Longer or steeper; good fitness needed.
S · Strenuous
Long and steep, often over rough ground.
VS · Very Strenuous
Demanding; for experienced walkers only.
